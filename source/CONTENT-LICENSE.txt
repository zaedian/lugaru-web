## Modifications in Lugaru-Web

Lugaru-Web contains preservation and compatibility changes to assets and maps
distributed under the licenses above.

Lugaru-Web modifications to bundled CC-licensed content include:

- Empire campaign map fixes to `Data/Maps/jendraz1`, `Data/Maps/jendraz2` and
  `Data/Maps/jendraz11`.
- Temple campaign map fixes to:
  - `Data/Maps/svenclimb`
  - `Data/Maps/svenheaven`
  - `Data/Maps/svenroad`
  - `Data/Maps/svendecoy`
  - `Data/Maps/svencouncil`
  - `Data/Maps/svendoor`
  - `Data/Maps/svenchasm`
  - `Data/Maps/svenfireice`
  - `Data/Maps/svenforest`
  - `Data/Maps/svenfort`
  - `Data/Maps/svenninja`
  - `Data/Maps/svenredbwafter`
  - `Data/Maps/svenruins`
  - `Data/Maps/svenshan`
  - `Data/Maps/sventemple`
  - `Data/Maps/sventemple2`
  - `Data/Maps/sventhrone`
- Seven Tasks map fix to `Data/Maps/task9` (removal of a duplicate dialogue
  attached to the wrong character, and speaker-name normalization).
- Campaign definition edits to `Data/Campaigns/main.txt`,
  `Data/Campaigns/empire.txt`, `Data/Campaigns/seventasks.txt` and
  `Data/Campaigns/temple.txt`: campaign start labels, the Temple final level
  label, and campaign ending text.
- `Data/Textures/Fur.png`, an RGBA conversion derived from the existing
  Wolfire `Fur.jpg` artwork for correct clothing tint behavior.
- Restoration of `Data/Textures/FurBW.jpg`, originally contributed to the
  OSS Lugaru project and distributed under CC-BY-SA 4.0 International.

These changes were made by the Lugaru-Web project in 2026 for browser
compatibility, preservation, restoration of intended historical behavior, or
removal of stale or erroneous campaign data found during release testing.

Temple campaign ending text: the `EndText` in `Data/Campaigns/temple.txt`
(beginning "Sven has slain the Water Temple master.") was newly written for
Lugaru-Web in 2026. It is not recovered original Temple campaign text. It
replaced a placeholder added by the OSS Lugaru project in 2017, which invited
players to propose an ending. As a modification of the Temple campaign file,
it is distributed under the applicable campaign-content license identified
above.
